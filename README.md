# Henrique Tamanini Silva Moschen

Ph.D. student in Animal Biology at the University of Brasília (UnB), based in the Toxinology Laboratory, Center for Molecular Biotechnology.

## Education

- Ph.D. in Animal Biology, University of Brasília (UnB) — in progress
- M.Sc. in Molecular Biology, University of Brasília (UnB)
- B.Sc. in Biological Sciences, Federal University of Espírito Santo (UFES)

## Research areas

Toxinology and bioprospecting, applied to venom peptide characterization and the rational design of bioinspired peptides for medical and biotechnological applications. Work covers venom peptide isolation and sequence analysis, protein structural modeling, membrane molecular dynamics, ion channel electrophysiology, and regulatory zebrafish embryo toxicity testing under OECD guidelines. Also involved in environmental metagenomics for bioremediation, as a member of the Environmental Metagenomics and Biotechnology Research Group (Prof. Ricardo Henrique Kruger, University of Brasília).

## Software and data

- **[ZebraFET](https://github.com/MoschenHTS/ZebraFET)** — desktop application for the OECD TG 236 Fish Embryo Acute Toxicity test. Covers experimental design, plate layout, daily well scoring, LC50/NOEC/LOEC and sublethal endpoint analysis, OECD validity checks, and automated report generation. [10.5281/zenodo.20183712](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20183712) · registered with Brazil's National Institute of Industrial Property (INPI BR512026001141-8)
- **[catanduba-simoni-venom-peptides](https://github.com/MoschenHTS/catanduba-simoni-venom-peptides)** — sequence and structural-biology pipeline for *Catanduba simoni* Kv4 gating-modifier venom peptides. Covers BLAST/MSA, structural co-folding, and membrane molecular dynamics. [10.5281/zenodo.22139140](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22139140)
