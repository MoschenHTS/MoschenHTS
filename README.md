# Henrique Tamanini Silva Moschen

Ph.D. student in Animal Biology at the University of Brasília (UnB), based in the Toxinology Laboratory, Center for Molecular Biotechnology.

## Education

- Ph.D. in Animal Biology, University of Brasília (UnB) — in progress
- M.Sc. in Molecular Biology, University of Brasília (UnB)
- B.Sc. in Biological Sciences, Federal University of Espírito Santo (UFES)

## Research areas

Toxinology, ecotoxicology, structural biology, and computational biology, applied to venom peptide characterization and regulatory aquatic toxicity testing. Work covers zebrafish embryo toxicity testing under OECD guidelines, venom peptide isolation and sequence analysis, protein structural modeling, membrane molecular dynamics, and ion channel electrophysiology.

## Software and data

- **[ZebraFET](https://github.com/MoschenHTS/ZebraFET)** — desktop application for the OECD TG 236 Fish Embryo Acute Toxicity test. Covers experimental design, plate layout, daily well scoring, LC50/NOEC/LOEC and sublethal endpoint analysis, OECD validity checks, and automated report generation. [10.5281/zenodo.20183712](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20183712)
- **[catanduba-simoni-venom-peptides](https://github.com/MoschenHTS/catanduba-simoni-venom-peptides)** — sequence and structural-biology pipeline for *Catanduba simoni* Kv4 gating-modifier venom peptides. Covers BLAST/MSA, structural co-folding, and membrane molecular dynamics. [10.5281/zenodo.22139140](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22139140)
